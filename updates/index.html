Linking Lost Jazz Shrines (LLJS) is a Weeksville Heritage Center and Semantic Lab at Pratt collaboration. The initial phase of LLJS was conducted from January 2019 through August 2020 with generous support from the Andrew W. Mellon Foundation “Collections as Data: Part to Whole” grant. LLJS was then generously supported by the Metropolitan New York Library Council (METRO) “Equity in Action” grant from February 2021 through October 2021.
